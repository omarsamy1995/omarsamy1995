## 🧬 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** specializing in **Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision**. My mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and production-grade intelligent systems.

From deploying real-time object recognition and facial analysis pipelines to fine-tuning **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and building **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** architectures, I engineer high-performance AI solutions that scale.

### 🎯 Key Highlights:
- 💡 **Core Specialization:** Deep Learning Architectures, Neural Networks, Computer Vision & Generative AI.
- 🚀 **Production Focus:** High-speed inferences using FastAPI, Docker, PyTorch/TensorFlow, and OpenCV/MediaPipe.
- 🧠 **Research & Applied AI:** Fine-tuning LLMs, prompt engineering, feature engineering, and dimensionality reduction (PCA, SVM, Clustering).
- 🤝 **Open Source & Collaboration:** Continuously building, documenting, and sharing scalable ML pipelines.
